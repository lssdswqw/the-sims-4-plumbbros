## 📖 About

🔹 **The Sims 4 — PlumbBros** is a powerful tool built to help you add realistic plumbing, water heating, and home utility management to The Sims 4. PlumbBros introduces working water systems where broken pipes cause flooding, cold showers, and repair bills.

🔸 Whether you're a seasoned professional or just getting started, **The Sims 4 — PlumbBros** delivers a clean, intuitive experience packed with everything you need — right out of the box. Join thousands of users who've already streamlined their The Sims 4 modding workflow. Download now and get started in minutes. 🚀

## 🚀 Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Water Systems | Working plumbing with pressure, temperature, and flow mechanics |
| Repair Events | Broken pipes cause flooding and require plumber visits |
| Utility Bills | Monthly water and heating bills based on actual household usage |
| Efficiency Upgrades | Upgrade to water-efficient appliances to reduce monthly costs |
